# Narrative Design Portfolio — Sai Srinivas Jammana

A portfolio exploring **narrative design, worldbuilding, character development, and interactive storytelling**.

## About

I'm Sai Srinivas Jammana, a game developer with a strong interest in narrative design and character-driven storytelling. Inspired by anime, manhwa, and manhua, I enjoy creating fictional worlds, exploring character motivations, and developing stories that unfold through gameplay.

I'm particularly interested in worlds that feel alive beyond their main characters, where side characters have their own motivations, established rules remain meaningful, and mysteries encourage players to discover what lies ahead.

## Portfolio

This website showcases original game concepts and narrative design explorations, with an emphasis on story structure, worldbuilding, character arcs, and potential gameplay integration.

### Featured Project: Coward's Arsenal

A dark-fantasy story concept about a coward raised by an assassin organization who escapes the life that shaped him. Haunted by fragmented memories of a future ending in destruction, he must grow stronger, gather powerful allies, and survive what is coming.

**Narrative focus:**
- Character development and personal growth
- Mystery, foreshadowing, and fragmented memories
- Worldbuilding and long-term story progression
- Connecting narrative ideas to potential gameplay scenarios

### Other Projects

The portfolio also explores additional concepts across dark fantasy, regression mysteries, and fantasy worldbuilding. These projects are works in progress and will be expanded as their narrative direction develops.

## Narrative Design Interests

- Character arcs and motivations
- Worldbuilding and internal consistency
- Quest concepts and player experience
- Mystery, foreshadowing, and narrative progression
- Meaningful choices and consequences
- Translating story ideas into interactive experiences

## Tools and Approach

I approach narrative design by exploring a concept's premise, characters, conflicts, and world rules, then considering how these elements could be communicated through gameplay, dialogue, quests, and player discovery.

My current portfolio includes original concepts and proposed narrative scenarios. Features described as proposals or concepts should not be interpreted as implemented gameplay unless explicitly stated.
