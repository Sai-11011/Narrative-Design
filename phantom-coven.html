Dark Fantasy / Narrative Design

Phantom Coven

A fallen necromantic bloodline, a changing gateway to the Abyss, and a 100-wave fight to restore a family the Solar Church wants erased.

Dark Fantasy·100-Wave Roguelike·Concept
CASE STUDY 02

01 / The Core Idea

The Premise

The protagonist is the last heir of a fallen necromantic bloodline. The Solar Church seeks to eradicate what it considers evil and pursues the protagonist because of their necromantic nature.

Phantom Coven is a dark-fantasy, 100-wave roguelike. The protagonist begins the first run alone, while restoring the family becomes a major motivation for permanent progression across attempts.

!

Development status
This is a concept in development. The page distinguishes confirmed gameplay structure from narrative opportunities and unresolved story decisions.

02 / World & Pressure

The Abyss and Its Changing Gates

The Abyss is a dangerous dimension whose gates appear at random locations in the real world. Because the gates change location, different creatures and groups can enter the Abyss over time rather than every run following one fixed route.

Souls do not dissipate in the Abyss. When sufficient soul energy gathers, it can form Eerie Orbs, the permanent progression currency used to unlock or resurrect family members and improve the protagonist and family members.

World rule

Souls can accumulate in the Abyss instead of dissipating.

Progression link

Eerie Orbs connect that soul accumulation to family restoration and permanent upgrades.

03 / Run Structure

Persistent Progression Across 100 Waves

Each run contains 100 enemy waves, with checkpoints every 10 waves. The first run begins with the protagonist alone. Family members are unlocked through permanent progression; once the sister is unlocked, the player can decide whether to include her in each new run, with the same team-selection principle applying to other unlocked family members.

Family members who die during a run cannot be resurrected during that run, but they remain available in later runs if they have already been permanently unlocked. Resources earned in previous runs carry forward, so attempts build on one another rather than resetting all progress.

The intended difficulty is challenging. An average player may need to unlock and upgrade the entire family to reach wave 100, while highly skilled players may succeed earlier.

04 / Character & Combat Design

The Family as a Combat System

Family restoration is both a narrative motivation and a team-building system. Each unlocked member changes the possible run composition and the way the player can respond to pressure.

The sister is a playable tank and protector unlocked through permanent progression; she is not available at the beginning of the game. Her confirmed abilities are:

01

Shield Bash

She dashes forward while striking with her shield.

02

Shield Slam

She creates dark vines and stuns nearby enemies.

03

Protective Shield

She provides 15% damage reduction while protecting others.

04

Boss Protection

During boss battles, she shares this protective effect with every family member.

05 / Resource Design

Two Layers of Progression

Eerie Orbs are a permanent currency associated with the accumulation of souls in the Abyss. They are used to unlock or resurrect family members and to fund permanent upgrades for the protagonist and family members.

Temporary combat energy is gained from defeated enemies and fills a bar during the current run. It is spent to summon combat support:

  1. 30%

    Mindless Mobs

    Pressing 1 summons mobs that tank damage and protect endangered family members.

    Run resource
  2. 50%

    Combat Units

    Pressing 2 summons combat-capable units such as equipped skeletons or dead paladins.

    Run resource
  3. 80%

    Guardian Monster

    Pressing 3 summons either of the family's two Guardian Monsters. Their stats are comparable to family members, and they excel at clearing groups.

    Run resource
  4. 90%

    Emergency Mobs

    Pressing 1 can still summon mindless mobs, reducing energy to 60%.

    Run resource
✦

Why separate the resources?

Eerie Orbs support long-term family restoration, while temporary energy creates moment-to-moment tactical decisions. These are gameplay systems, not events that necessarily occur in the story.

06 / Narrative Direction

Narrative Opportunities

The Abyss's persistent souls create an opportunity for environmental storytelling. Memories of those who died there could shape how the player understands the dimension, its gates, and the history surrounding the family.

This is a direction under consideration, not a confirmed memory system or established body of lore. The Solar Church remains the larger external threat, while the protagonist's necromantic abilities and family history keep them in conflict with it.

07 / Open Design Questions

Design Challenges and Next Steps

The central challenge is balancing a difficult 100-wave structure with progression that feels meaningful without making every run feel identical. Checkpoints, persistent resources, team selection, and family-member deaths need to create pressure while still giving the player reasons to attempt another run.

The next steps are to define how gates and wave composition create variety, clarify the family's full upgrade structure, and decide how much of the Abyss's soul-based environmental storytelling belongs in the finished concept. The Church's deeper motives, any final confrontation, and a definitive ending remain unresolved.
