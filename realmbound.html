Original Concept / Worldbuilding & Systems Design

RealmBound

A collectible fantasy card game built around one interconnected world, where every card introduces a character, a culture, or a story worth discovering.

Fantasy TCG·Worldbuilding·Draft / Work in Progress
CASE STUDY 04

01 / The Core Idea

A World Told Through Cards

RealmBound is a collectible fantasy trading card game set in one shared world. Each card represents an individual with a place in that world—not merely a unit with statistics.

The project's central goal is to make players curious about who a character is, what they do, and how they fit into the wider setting. Heroes, legendary figures, and ordinary people all contribute to the world.

!

Development status
RealmBound has foundational worldbuilding and design documents, alongside an evolving early ruleset. Card content, balance, presentation, and implementation remain works in progress.

02 / World Foundation

One Source, Many Interpretations

Essence is the shared foundation of life and power across the world. Different civilizations interpret and use it in different ways: through ritual and arcane study, martial discipline, engineered constructs, divine traditions, or biological transformation.

This shared foundation helps the world feel connected without making every culture identical. Civilizations can have distinct histories, visual identities, values, and approaches to conflict while still belonging to the same setting.

Shared foundation

Essence connects the world's different forms of power.

Cultural distinction

Civilizations express Essence through different beliefs, practices, and technologies.

03 / Design Pillars

Story and Gameplay Support Each Other

01

Every Card Has an Identity

Principle
Each character has a life, purpose, and place in the world, whether a legendary hero or an ordinary worker.
Design implication
Begin with who the character is and what they do before deciding their combat role.
Player experience
Collecting becomes a way to discover the setting, not only a search for stronger cards.
02

Mechanics Express Character

Principle
A character's abilities should reinforce their identity rather than feel interchangeable.
Design implication
A protector should express protection through play; a scholar might express research or discovery.
Trade-off
Specific abilities can create balance and rules complexity, so identity must be expressed with readable mechanics.
03

Strategic Depth from Interactions

Principle
Keep individual cards approachable and let interesting decisions emerge from how cards interact.
Design implication
Prefer a clear primary role over loading one character with unrelated effects.
Goal
Make deck decisions and battlefield choices meaningful without requiring long blocks of rules text.
04

Gameplay Comes Before Lore

Principle
Lore should inspire gameplay, but should not prevent a mechanic from being understandable, enjoyable, or practical to implement.
Design implication
If a rule creates poor gameplay, revise the worldbuilding rather than preserving complexity only for lore consistency.

04 / World Structure

A Shared Setting with Distinct Civilizations

The current foundation includes groups such as Beastfolk, the Clockwork Dominion, Arcane Mystics, Dragon Kin, and Divine Orders, alongside other civilizations still being refined. Civilizations shape culture, history, architecture, and traditions; species and individual characters can exist within this wider social structure.

The world can expand into new regions and societies without resetting the setting each time. New cards should deepen existing connections or introduce a distinct part of the same world.

↗

Open development work
The final civilization roster, region map, chronology, and relationships between major groups still need consolidation across the project's documents.

05 / Gameplay Direction

Collect, Build, Battle, Discover

RealmBound's gameplay direction combines collectible card acquisition, deck construction, and tactical battles. Cards are intended to work together through clear roles and interactions, while their identities and stories provide context for their abilities.

Collection

Discover characters and other cards from the shared world.

Deck building

Combine card roles and synergies into a strategy that reflects the player's choices.

Battle

Make tactical decisions using positioning, resources, character abilities, and interactions.

Discovery

Learn more about the world through the characters and civilizations in the collection.

Turn structure, balance values, collection economy, and long-term progression remain subject to iteration and should be presented as prototype rules until validated.

07 / Next Steps

Make the Foundation Consistent

The next priority is to bring the worldbuilding, card identity rules, and prototype rules into one consistent reference. From there, a small set of representative cards can test whether the project's principles work in actual play.

✦

Design question to validate

Can players recognize a character's identity from both its story and gameplay, and can a small group of simple cards create meaningful tactical choices together?
